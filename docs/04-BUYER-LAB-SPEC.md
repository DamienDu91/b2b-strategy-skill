# Buyer Lab
Simulate organizations, not consumer demographics. Inputs: sector, country, employee band, governance, purchasing maturity, site autonomy, budget relative to buyer capacity, purchase type (goods/services/software), criticality, new task/modified rebuy/straight rebuy, incumbent, alternatives. Generate correlated scenarios with reproducible seed and explicit assumptions. Roles depend on delegated authority, risk and context, not headcount alone. Model technical, quality, security, financial and procurement vetoes; no-decision is always available. Stages may repeat or be skipped. Modes: small synthetic panel, buying committee, competitive offer comparison. Outputs: objections, missing evidence, gates and real-world validation plan. No inferred conversion probability or sales cycle prediction. V1 qualitative; calibrated prediction deferred. Test contrast pairs and price/criticality sensitivity.
