# PRD
Users: SME leaders, marketing and sales leaders, consultants. Jobs: compare strategic options, stress-test an offer, build an investment case, choose a GTM plan. Quick mode asks only decision-critical questions; deeper mode documents evidence and scenarios. Output: executive decision memo, alternatives including status quo, assumptions, calculations, risks, experiments and owners. Acceptance: no invented evidence, no synthetic purchase forecasts, missing critical inputs block numerical conclusions. FR/EN. Out of scope: CRM integration, autonomous action, accounting and predictive calibration.
