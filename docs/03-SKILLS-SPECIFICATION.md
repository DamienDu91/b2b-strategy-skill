# Eight skills
Business Discovery: brief and gaps. Strategic Board: options and trade-offs. Market Intelligence: dated sources and competitor evidence. Buyer Lab: synthetic organizational buying scenarios and objections. Business Finance: deterministic incremental cash-flow and unit economics. Commercial Strategy: offer, positioning, price, GTM. Independent Reviewer: contradiction, falsification and security checks. Decision & Execution: conditional recommendation, experiments and decision owners. Each skill takes a versioned brief and emits structured evidence-linked findings; reviewer can block unsupported conclusions.
